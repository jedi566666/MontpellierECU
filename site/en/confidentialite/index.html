Montpellier ECU · Base

Your data. Clearly explained.

Local storage

The last viewed address and listing drafts are stored in your browser. Clear the Wallet session, delete individual drafts or clear the website data in your browser to remove them.

External services

Base RPC receives the public address when balances are requested. GeckoTerminal, DexScreener and Blockscout supply market data and transfers. These services and Cloudflare receive technical request data, including IP addresses. No advertising or analytics script is included.

Wallet

You choose which accounts to share with the site. No recovery phrase, private key or signing secret is requested. Reading a balance does not create a transaction.
