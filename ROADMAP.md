# Public Roadmap

[Version française](ROADMAP_FR.md)

This roadmap is outcome-based. Dates are intentionally not promised until resources and dependencies are confirmed.

## Phase Alpha — Foundations

**Objective:** make the project understandable, verifiable and safer to approach.

- Maintain the official website and canonical links.
- Publish bilingual reference documentation.
- Keep contract, network and supply data consistent across platforms.
- Document market risks and liquidity principles.
- Improve security reporting and incident procedures.
- Clearly separate live, beta and proposed products.

**Exit criteria**

- No contradictory core information across official channels.
- Public documentation covers the token, ecosystem, risks and security.
- Official interfaces identify the canonical contract.

## Phase Bravo — Real usage

**Objective:** demonstrate that MTP can be used, not only held.

- Populate the Marketplace with genuine goods and services.
- Simplify the user path from discovery to first transaction.
- Add clear listing, search and status mechanisms.
- Establish basic seller reputation and moderation rules.
- Publish anonymised activity indicators where technically reliable.

**Exit criteria**

- Repeated real-world marketplace usage.
- Documented transaction flow.
- A functioning support and moderation process.

## Phase Charlie — Pilot partners

**Objective:** validate MTP with a limited number of credible participants.

- Recruit local and remote pilot users.
- Test merchant or service-provider benefits.
- Offer practical onboarding material.
- Collect structured feedback.
- Publish only confirmed partnerships.

**Exit criteria**

- Several active pilot participants.
- At least one repeatable partner use case.
- Measurable feedback translated into product changes.

## Phase Delta — Sustainable ecosystem revenue

**Objective:** fund operations through services rather than token-price promises.

Potential models, subject to technical and legal review:

- paid marketplace visibility;
- professional seller options;
- digital support services;
- transparent service fees;
- software customisation or integration;
- sponsored but clearly labelled placements.

**Exit criteria**

- A documented source of non-speculative revenue.
- Transparent pricing and terms.
- Operational costs tracked separately from token valuation.

## Phase Echo — Advanced participation

**Objective:** introduce advanced mechanisms only after proven demand.

Possible areas:

- community badges;
- access rights;
- contributor recognition;
- limited governance experiments;
- loyalty mechanisms;
- staking only after economic, security and legal assessment.

**Exit criteria**

- Clear user need.
- Public specification.
- Security review.
- No misleading promise of passive or guaranteed income.

## Decision rule

A phase is completed by evidence, not by announcement.
