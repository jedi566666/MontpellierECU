## Supply and ownership

Blockchain balances are public and may be inspected independently. The project does not publish promotional wallet rankings or personal-security-sensitive wallet profiles.

A high concentration of supply creates material risks, including governance dependence, market impact and counterparty perception. MTP does not conceal this structural fact. Any future distribution should be gradual, documented by purpose and designed to support actual ecosystem use rather than artificial activity.

## Intended allocation functions

Tokens controlled by the project may be used progressively for purposes such as:

- marketplace circulation;
- user onboarding;
- service payments;
- partner pilots;
- liquidity provision;
- contributor recognition;
- ecosystem operations.

This list describes possible functions, not binding allocations or guaranteed distributions.

## No guaranteed value

MTP has no guaranteed redemption value, fixed price or promised appreciation. Market value can fluctuate significantly and available liquidity may be limited.

## Change control

Any material change to publicly stated token parameters or distribution policy should be recorded in this repository's changelog.
