# Liquidity Principles

[Version française](LIQUIDITY_FR.md)

## Purpose

Liquidity should make legitimate buying, selling and ecosystem transactions easier. It must not be presented as a tool for guaranteeing a rising price.

## Operating principles

1. **Gradual funding** — additions should remain compatible with the project's real budget.
2. **No artificial-volume strategy** — self-trading, coordinated deceptive activity and fabricated demand are incompatible with this plan.
3. **Public identification** — official pools may be referenced publicly once their addresses and status have been independently verified.
4. **Risk awareness** — liquidity providers face price variation, impermanent loss, smart-contract risk and the risk of low market depth.
5. **No emergency promises** — the project does not promise to defend a particular price or buy every sale.
6. **Separation of functions** — operating funds, personal funds and liquidity positions should be tracked distinctly where practical.

## Lessons from small liquidity positions

Even a small liquidity position can lose value relative to simply holding the two assets. A pool balance changing does not automatically mean that funds were stolen; it may result from swaps, price movements, fees and impermanent loss. Every position should be reviewed through the pool contract and the connected wallet history.
