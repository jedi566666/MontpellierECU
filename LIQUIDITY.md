# Liquidity

Liquidity enables exchanges between MTP and other assets. It can be limited, fragmented or volatile.

## Principles

- Do not confuse displayed price with guaranteed realizable value.
- Verify the network, pair address, token contract and DEX before swapping.
- Expect slippage when liquidity is low.
- Never assume that a pool will remain available or unchanged.
- Public communication should describe liquidity factually and avoid claims of guaranteed stability.

Pool information may change. Use MTP Live and recognized blockchain explorers to verify current data before any transaction.
