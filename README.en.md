# MTP — Montpellier ECU

**Born in Occitania, intended for use around the world.**

MTP is a crypto asset on Base, originating in Montpellier. We are progressively building practical uses around digital tools, voluntary exchanges and creative work.

**Montpellier is its origin, not its boundary.** Worldwide reach is an ambition, not a claim of universal acceptance today.

[Français](README.md) · [Website](https://mtptoken.pages.dev/) · [Base contract](https://basescan.org/token/0x50626097a780881d3dFf1Ff97579e6dAF965366B) · [MTP Live](https://mtptoken.pages.dev/live/) · [MTP App](https://github.com/jedi566666/MontpellierECU/releases) · [Documentation](docs/README.md)

## Understand MTP in one minute
- An ERC-20 token on Base with a fixed supply of 21 million MTP.
- A project exploring useful exchanges involving objects, time, skills and creations.
- Today: inspect a public address, view available market data, prepare a local listing draft and join the community.
- In development: integrated payments, server-published listings and merchant workflows.
- Verify claims through the contract, reproducible evidence and documented limitations.

## Why Montpellier?
Montpellier is the starting point, Occitania the identity, and the wider world the horizon. This is an independent project; its name does not imply support from the city or another institution.

## Argent 0 — value exists before money
An available hour, a reusable object, a skill to pass on or a creation to share already has value.

**Time · Skills · Knowledge · Objects · Creation · Services · Reuse**

Argent 0 means making good use of existing resources, reducing unnecessary spending and recognising human contributions. It does not require everything to be free. Work can be paid, exchanges must be consensual and real costs remain visible.

MTP may become a tool for circulating that value. Its usefulness must be demonstrated with participants.

**UTILITY > SPECULATION**

## An open path to practical uses
Target journey, not an already integrated service chain:

**MTP → MTP App → Payment → Marketplace → De l’écran à l’abandon → Montpellier → Occitania → World**

De l’écran à l’abandon is the first major intended creative ecosystem: a graphic novel and a game in development. These remain separate projects. Holding MTP does not automatically grant access to them or to their revenue.

## Utility roadmap
1. **De l’écran à l’abandon:** validated creative deliveries and a first voluntary use.
2. **MTP App:** reliable distribution, working links and documented limitations.
3. **Marketplace:** useful listings, then publication, moderation and responsibilities.
4. **Montpellier:** experiments and feedback from volunteers.
5. **Occitania:** extend uses that have demonstrated value.
6. **France:** adapt to participants and their needs.
7. **World:** maintain languages and workflows where there is real demand.

Each phase depends on verifiable results, available resources and human validation. No partners or merchants are claimed as secured by this roadmap.
